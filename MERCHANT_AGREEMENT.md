# Takeer Merchant Marketplace Agreement

**Operator:** AVLY TECH GROUP LIMITED

## 1. Marketplace role

Takeer provides marketplace, catalog, ordering, fulfillment coordination, customer-support, dispute-review, and trust-and-safety technology. The merchant remains the seller of record for its products and services unless the parties expressly agree otherwise in writing. The merchant is responsible for lawful listings, product quality, delivery, service performance, customer communications, taxes, and required licences.

Takeer does not issue electronic money, operate a deposit or stored-value account, provide a general-purpose wallet, or take custody of marketplace funds.

## 2. Payment service providers

Buyer collections, payment authentication, custody within the approved payment structure, refunds, reversals, settlement, and seller payouts are performed by the licensed payment service provider shown at checkout. The merchant must complete any provider onboarding, KYC/KYB, beneficiary verification, sanctions screening, and processing terms required by that provider.

Amounts displayed in Takeer as pending, eligible, submitted, completed, refunded, or disputed are order-specific commerce and provider-status records. They are not a transferable balance, deposit, credit, or money held by Takeer for the merchant. Takeer does not accept arbitrary withdrawal amounts, cash-out instructions, transfers between users, or spending of marketplace earnings inside the platform.

Takeer may calculate its disclosed marketplace fee and use an approved PSP split or settlement instruction. Takeer will not collect seller principal into an operating account and later pay the seller from unrelated company funds.

## 3. Fulfillment, release, and payout

The merchant must provide the fulfillment evidence and customer support required by the order and its disclosed policy. Takeer may mark an order eligible to request a provider payout after the applicable fulfillment, review, fraud, dispute, and compliance checks. Only the PSP may execute the seller payout, and a payout must be linked to the specific order allocation or PSP-approved batch. A payout is complete only after authenticated PSP evidence confirms completion.

Takeer may pause a release request for a documented dispute, fraud, legal, provider, or compliance reason. A pause is an order-state control and does not create a Takeer-held custody account.

## 3A. Self-pickup cancellation amount

Where the merchant enables self-pickup, the merchant must disclose a reasonable cancellation percentage in the pickup location settings. The percentage, pickup window, and location shown before payment are snapshotted on the order and cannot be changed for that order by a later settings edit. If a buyer does not collect by the agreed deadline and the order is cancelled through the pickup workflow, the disclosed percentage of the authenticated amount paid may be retained as an order-specific cancellation amount. The merchant must not demand an additional offline penalty through Takeer chat.

The cancellation amount is not a Takeer wallet credit. Takeer records the amount and requests the corresponding buyer refund from the licensed PSP. Any retained merchant allocation must be handled and confirmed by the PSP under the approved marketplace settlement contract; Takeer does not create a merchant ledger credit or pay it from unrelated operating funds.

## 4. Refunds, reversals, and chargebacks

Takeer may decide marketplace eligibility under the disclosed refund and dispute policy and submit an idempotent provider instruction. The PSP executes the refund or reversal. The merchant agrees to cooperate with evidence requests and accepts the provider and contract treatment of refunds, reversals, chargebacks, and any lawful recovery from future order allocations.

## 5. Merchant warranties and data

The merchant warrants that its listings, products, services, claims, fulfilment, and customer-data use comply with applicable law, platform policies, and provider requirements. Customer data supplied for order fulfilment must be used only for that purpose unless the customer gives another lawful permission. The merchant must maintain appropriate security and promptly report suspected fraud, account compromise, data incidents, or payment irregularities.

## 6. Complaints and governing law

The parties will follow the platform complaints and redress process and first attempt good-faith resolution. This agreement is subject to the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania, with the final dispute forum and any mandatory consumer rights confirmed by counsel before execution.

## 7. Clickwrap evidence

The merchant accepts this agreement only through an affirmative, unticked acceptance action that records the document version, content hash, timestamp, user, merchant, IP address, user agent, locale, and acceptance action. Material changes require re-acceptance before new sales or provider payouts.
