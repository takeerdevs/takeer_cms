# Takeer Buyer Terms

Takeer is a marketplace and order-fulfillment technology platform. Independent sellers remain responsible for their goods and services. Licensed payment service providers process collections, refunds, reversals, settlement, and seller payouts. Takeer does not issue a wallet, deposit, stored value, or electronic money. Order-status amounts are not transferable balances. Product, delivery, refund, dispute, privacy, and complaint disclosures apply before payment.

## Self-pickup and non-collection

For a self-pickup order, checkout shows the pickup location, requested pickup window, and any cancellation penalty before payment. If the buyer does not collect by the agreed deadline and the order is cancelled under the pickup policy, the disclosed percentage of the amount actually paid may be retained as the order's pickup cancellation amount. The order records the percentage, amount retained, and refund amount that applied when the order was cancelled.

The cancellation amount is not a Takeer wallet credit or a Takeer payment-service fee. Any retained seller allocation and any buyer refund are handled through the licensed PSP's approved marketplace settlement and refund process. Takeer does not pay the seller from unrelated company funds. The refund amount is the amount paid less the disclosed cancellation amount, subject to authenticated PSP processing. A written extension or delivery-conversion agreement recorded in the order chat changes the applicable pickup deadline and is considered before cancellation.
