# Payment and PSP Processing Terms

The licensed payment service provider named at checkout controls payment authentication and the approved collection and settlement structure. The provider executes refunds and pays verified seller beneficiaries. Takeer may send order-specific payment, release, refund, and reconciliation instructions but does not custody funds or pay sellers from unrelated operating cash. Provider terms, limits, outages, verification, chargebacks, and reconciliation rules apply.

For a self-pickup non-collection cancellation, Takeer sends the PSP the order-specific refund amount calculated from the pickup policy snapshot. The PSP is responsible for executing and reporting the refund and for applying any retained cancellation amount or seller allocation only under its approved marketplace settlement contract. Takeer does not treat the retained amount as a wallet, deposit, or internal merchant balance.
