# Refund, Return, Cancellation, and Dispute Policy

Takeer reviews marketplace eligibility using the order policy and evidence. An approved refund or reversal is submitted as an idempotent provider instruction; the licensed PSP executes the money movement. Delivery evidence, digital access, service completion, fraud, chargeback, seller conduct, and provider status affect the outcome. No Takeer wallet debit or credit is created.

## Pickup cancellation after non-collection

For self-pickup, the pickup location's cancellation percentage is shown before payment and snapshotted on the order. If the buyer does not collect by the agreed deadline and the merchant cancels the order under the pickup workflow, the disclosed percentage is calculated against the authenticated amount paid. The remaining amount is the buyer's refund request; the retained amount is an order-specific cancellation amount, not a Takeer-held balance.

Takeer records the decision and, after any required authorised review, sends the exact refund amount to the licensed PSP. The PSP must execute, confirm, or reject that refund through the original payment or another approved route and must handle any retained seller allocation under its marketplace settlement contract. Takeer never marks a refund complete merely because a cancellation or admin approval occurred. Extensions, delivery conversion, merchant-caused cancellation, provider failure, disputes, and evidence review can change or pause the outcome.
